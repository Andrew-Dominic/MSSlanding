Frequently asked questions

The questions our support team hears most.

Who is Saarthi?
Saarthi is the AI companion at the heart of MSS. Instead of filling lengthy forms, you simply speak or type in English or Tamil, and Saarthi guides you through profile creation, explains scheme benefits in simple language, and continuously refines your recommendations through progressive profiling.
How does the AI scheme matching work?
MSS's AI recommendation engine analyses your demographic data, location, and business or education profile against the eligibility rules of 217 Central and State government schemes in real-time. It delivers a ranked list with a transparent confidence score and a "Why You Qualify" breakdown for each matched scheme.
Can I use MSS offline?
Yes. MSS includes a bundled offline scheme database (government_schemes.json) with all 217 schemes so you can browse and receive recommendations even without internet access. Direct application links do require connectivity to reach official government portals.
Can I apply for schemes directly through MSS?
MSS serves as an AI-powered discovery and guidance platform. It generates a precise document checklist for each scheme and provides direct links to the official government portals for registration and submission — keeping you on official channels at all times.
Is the app free to use?
Yes, MSS is entirely free to download and use. Our mission is to bridge the information gap between government initiatives and potential beneficiaries and make government support programmes accessible to all.
